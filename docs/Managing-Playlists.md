# Managing playlists

<img src="images/screens/playlists.png" width="300" alt="Playlists">

Each playlist card shows:
- the playlist type
- how many channels, movies and series it has
- when it was last updated
- how many guide entries are stored
- the account expiry date, for Xtream accounts
- the maximum number of connections, when the provider reports it or you've entered it. See [Maximum connections](Adding-Playlists.md#maximum-connections).

## Switching playlists
With more than one playlist, **Live TV**, **Movies** and **Series** each choose their own: the playlists button at the top of the categories (on TV, the top of the categories column or panel) offers **All playlists** and each playlist.
- **All playlists** shows them together. Categories with the same name, such as News in two playlists, become one. Each channel, movie or series says which playlist it's from, and plays from it.
- **A playlist** shows just that one.

**Use this playlist** on a card makes it the active playlist: the one the Library shows, and the one Live TV, Movies and Series all go back to showing. On a phone, the Library tab also switches it with the playlist name under the Tellycine logo.

A movie or series page, and a channel's details, say which playlist it's from whenever you have more than one.

## Refreshing
- **Refresh** reloads the playlist and its TV guide. **Stop** cancels a refresh in progress.
- **⋮ → Update TV guide only** reloads just the guide.
- **Automatic refresh:** when the app starts, playlists older than a day and guides older than 12 hours are refreshed. You can turn this off in [Settings](Settings.md).

A refresh replaces the playlist in a single step. You keep seeing the old channels until the new ones are ready, and a failed refresh never leaves a half-empty playlist.

## Air dates from TVmaze
With **Air dates from TVmaze** turned on in [Settings](Settings.md), a section below the playlists shows how looking series up is going: how many it has done while it works, then how many series TVmaze found, when it last checked and how many it didn't find. **Check now** checks again straight away. See [Movies & series](Movies-and-Series.md#air-dates-from-tvmaze).

## Choosing categories to show
Open **⋮ → Categories**, or **Choose categories to show** in the playlist editor.

<img src="images/screens/category-filter.png" width="300" alt="Category filter">

- **Separate tabs** for Live TV, Movies and Series.
- **Ticking:** untick a category to hide it everywhere in that playlist: browsing, "All", search, favourites and channel switching in the player.
- **Filter box:** narrows the list. **Tick all** and **Untick all** then apply to the matching categories, for example everything containing "Arabic".
- **No refresh needed:** changes apply instantly, and your choices survive refreshes.
- **New categories** your provider adds later start ticked.

## Editing and deleting
- **⋮ → Edit:** change the address, login, maximum connections, guide URLs, user agent or the Movies & series switch. Saving reloads the playlist.
- **⋮ → Delete:** removes the playlist with its channels, guide, favourites and watch history from your device.
