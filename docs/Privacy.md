# Privacy

- **What's stored, and where:** Mosaic keeps your playlists, login details, TV guide, favourites, watch history and settings **only on your device**. They're removed when you delete the playlist or uninstall the app.
- **No accounts or tracking:** Mosaic has no account system, analytics, ads or tracking.
- **Who Mosaic connects to:** only the servers you add (your playlist, guide and Xtream or Dispatcharr servers), plus the artwork URLs those servers provide. Casting uses Google's Cast service between your phone and your TV.
- **Dispatcharr:** only your API key is stored, never your web password.
- **Sharing:** shared stream URLs and stream URLs passed to external players include your provider login details, so only share them with people you trust.
