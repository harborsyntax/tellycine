# Search

Tap the search icon in the top bar of the Live TV, Movies, Series or Library tab.

<img src="images/screens/search.png" width="300" alt="Search">

- **What it searches:** channel, movie and series names in the active playlist. Type at least two letters.
- **Filters:** use the chips to search **All**, **Live TV**, **Movies** or **Series**.
- **Results:** channels play straight away; movies and series open their details.
- **Hidden categories** are excluded. See [Managing playlists](Managing-Playlists.md).
