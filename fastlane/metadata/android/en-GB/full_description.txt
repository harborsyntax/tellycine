Tellycine is a player for IPTV playlists you already have. Add your provider's playlist and TV guide, then watch live TV, movies and series on your phone, tablet and Android TV.

Tellycine doesn't include, sell or recommend any channels, streams or other content. You need a playlist from a provider you subscribe to, or one of your own.

YOUR PLAYLISTS
• M3U and M3U8 playlists, from a link or a file on your device
• Xtream Codes-compatible logins and Dispatcharr servers
• As many playlists as you like, and switch between them
• XMLTV TV guide data

LIVE TV
• A traditional grid TV guide, or a channel list showing what's on now
• Categories and favourites
• Catch-up, where your provider offers it
• Quick channel switching, picture-in-picture and Chromecast

ANDROID TV AND GOOGLE TV
• A full interface for the remote, with a grid guide and a categories column
• Multi-view: up to four live channels at once, with sound and volume per channel and three layouts
• A mini player keeps your channel playing while you browse the guide
• Five colour themes made for big screens
• Add a playlist from your phone by scanning a QR code
• Voice search

MOVIES AND SERIES
• Browse your provider's movies and series by category
• Continue watching, resume where you left off, and download for offline viewing
• Search across channels, the TV guide, movies and series

THE PLAYER
• Audio and subtitle tracks, and picture fit options
• Stats for nerds: formats, HDR, decoders, download rate and buffer
• Open a stream in another player if you prefer

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, ads, analytics or tracking
• Your playlists, logins and watch history stay on your device
• Tellycine only connects to the servers you add
