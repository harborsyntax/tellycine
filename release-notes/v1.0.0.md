## Tellycine 1.0.0

The first release of Tellycine, an IPTV player for Android phones and tablets.

- **Your sources:** M3U/M3U8 links, local M3U files, Xtream Codes accounts and Dispatcharr servers, with as many playlists as you like. Pick a user agent per playlist, and see each account's connection limit.
- **Live TV:** an XMLTV guide with what's on now, a full schedule for every channel, and a traditional grid guide in landscape. Search channels and programmes.
- **Catch-up TV:** replay past programmes, or restart the current one.
- **Movies and series:** posters, details, seasons and episodes, and resume where you left off.
- **Library:** favourites, continue watching and recently watched, with sorting, grouping and long-press to remove.
- **Downloads:** save movies and episodes to watch offline.
- **Chromecast,** including Dispatcharr live channels, plus **Open with…** any other video player.
- **Dispatcharr:** DVR recordings, and sharing source stream URLs.
- **Material 3 design:** light and dark themes, and layouts for phones, tablets and foldables.
