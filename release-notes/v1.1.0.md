## What's new in 1.1.0

**Tube is now Mosaic**, with a new icon (including an Android 13+ themed icon), a new logo and an amber colour palette.

> Mosaic installs as a new app, alongside Tube 1.0.0. Re-add your playlists in Mosaic, then uninstall Tube.

- **Catch-up TV:** replay past programmes, or restart the current one, from a channel's guide on channels with an archive. Supports Xtream Codes timeshift and M3U `catchup` / `catchup-source`.
- **Dispatcharr support:** Dispatcharr servers are detected automatically, catch-up works with them, and their placeholder expiry date is hidden.
- **Category filters:** tick or untick each playlist's Live TV, Movies and Series categories to hide them everywhere. Your choices survive refreshes.
- **Movies & series switch:** turn VOD off per playlist to skip downloading it and hide the Movies and Series tabs.
