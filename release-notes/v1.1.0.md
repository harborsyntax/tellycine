## Tellycine 1.1.0: now on Android TV

Tellycine now runs on Android TV and Google TV, with an interface made for the remote. It's the same app: install this APK on your TV and it opens the TV interface, with its own banner in the launcher.

- **A TV guide for the remote:** channels down the side and time across the top. Move through programmes with the arrows, go back in time for catch-up, and hold OK for a channel's schedule, favourites, recording and more.
- **Channel surfing:** up/down or CH+/CH− change channel, number keys jump to a channel, and one press flips back to the channel you were watching before. The banner shows what's on now and next.
- **Everything else from the phone app:** movies and series, details and episodes, the Library, search (with voice), downloads, Dispatcharr recordings, playlists, categories and settings.
- **Add a playlist from your phone:** scan a QR code on the TV and type the details, or send a playlist file, from your phone.
- **Stream URLs as QR codes,** so you can open them on your phone.
- **TV settings:** open on the last channel, and match the TV's refresh rate to the video (smoother 25/50 fps channels).

On a TV, install with the Downloader app from `github.com/harborsyntax/tellycine/releases/latest/download/tellycine.apk`. See [Android TV](https://github.com/harborsyntax/tellycine/blob/main/docs/Android-TV.md) for details.
