## Tellycine 1.1.1: Android TV fixes

- **Live TV no longer freezes on some TVs.** Xtream live channels could freeze as soon as they started on Android TV unless "Prefer HLS for Xtream live TV" was turned on. They now play with the setting off.
- **Player buttons are no longer cut off.** The first and last buttons along the bottom of the TV player had their rounded ends clipped when highlighted.
