## Tellycine 1.1.2: live TV fix for Android TV

- **Another fix for live TV freezing on some TVs.** On some Android TV devices, Xtream live channels played sound but the picture froze after a second unless "Prefer HLS for Xtream live TV" was turned on. Live TV streams are now read on a TV the same way as HLS streams, which played smoothly on those TVs.
