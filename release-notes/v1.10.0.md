## Tellycine 1.10.0: sort movies and series, and a nudge towards Xtream Codes

- **Sort movies and series.** A new sort button (at the top of the screen; on a TV, beside the category button) puts titles in another order, remembered separately for Movies and Series:
  - **Date added**, newest first. Xtream Codes providers say when they added each movie; for M3U playlists and most series, it's when Tellycine first saw the title, so from the next refresh on, new arrivals come to the top.
  - **Release date**, newest first, from the provider's release date or year (in M3U playlists, a year in brackets in the title, as in "Film Name (2023)").
  - **Latest episode** for series, from Xtream Codes providers.
  - **A–Z**, and **Provider order** as before.
- **Is there an Xtream Codes login?** When you add an M3U link, Tellycine now asks its server whether it also offers Xtream Codes, and suggests switching when it does, with the login filled in if the link's login works. Xtream Codes gives movie and series details, catch-up and dates to sort by.
- Long lists of movies and series open faster, sorted or not.

Your playlists, favourites and history are kept: the app updates its database in place. Movies and series get their dates at the next refresh of each playlist.
