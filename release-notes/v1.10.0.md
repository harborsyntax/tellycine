## What's new in 1.10.0

**A traditional TV guide in landscape**

- Turn your phone or tablet sideways on **Live TV** and tap the **guide** icon in the top bar. You get a grid with channels down the left, time across the top, and a line marking the current time. Tap the **list** icon to switch back. Mosaic remembers your choice.
- Swipe up and down through channels and sideways through time, and tap **Now** to jump back.
- **Tap a programme:**
  - **On now:** watches the channel.
  - **Already shown,** on catch-up channels: replays it.
  - **Coming up:** opens the channel's guide, where Dispatcharr playlists can record it. Programmes set to record show a red dot.

**More room in landscape**

- In landscape, the tabs now sit in a rail down the side instead of a bar along the bottom, leaving more height for channels and the guide.

If you're on 1.9.1, this update installs straight over the top.
