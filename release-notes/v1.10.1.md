## What's new in 1.10.1

**Chromecast fixes**

- **Live TV keeps playing.** Live channels used to stutter and stop on the Chromecast after a minute or two, because they were sent as if they were ordinary videos. They're now sent as live streams, so the Chromecast keeps up with the broadcast.
- **A warning when a movie's sound won't cast.** Many movies, especially MKV files, have Dolby or DTS soundtracks. Chromecast can't play these itself: it passes Dolby to your TV or soundbar, and doesn't support DTS at all. Before casting a movie or episode, Mosaic checks its soundtracks. If the sound probably won't play on the TV, it tells you on the casting screen, along with any other soundtrack the file has.

If you're on 1.10.0, this update installs straight over the top.
