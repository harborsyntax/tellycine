## Tellycine 1.10.1: sort either way round

- **Either way round.** Every order in Movies and Series now works both ways. The current one has an arrow beside it, down for newest first or Z to A, and up for oldest first or A to Z. Press it again to turn it round; the menu stays open so you can see the arrow change.
- **Newest first to start with.** Movies and Series now open on **Date added**, newest first, until you choose another order. After that they keep your last choice, as before. If you had already chosen an order in 1.10.0, it's kept.
- **Title** replaces **A–Z** in the list, since it now goes from Z to A as well.
- **Back to the top.** After changing the order (or on a TV, the category), the list now starts from the top. Before, it could stay scrolled down to wherever the title that was first ended up.
- Oldest first keeps titles without a date at the end, and opens as quickly as newest first.
