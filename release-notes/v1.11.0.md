## Tellycine 1.11.0: when episodes aired, from TVmaze

- **Air dates from TVmaze (optional).** Turn on **Air dates from TVmaze** in Settings to look your series up on TVmaze.com, a free TV database. Series then get a **Last aired** sort: by when their latest episode was broadcast, rather than when your provider added it. It's off unless you turn it on, as it sends your series' names to TVmaze (nothing else).
  - The first look-up takes a while with many series. After that, Tellycine only asks about new series, shows TVmaze has changed, shows with a new episode out since, and series that gained episodes.
  - The **Playlists** page shows how it's going, how many series were found, and has **Check now**.
- **Latest episode for M3U playlists too.** Tellycine now notes when a series gains episodes at a refresh, so **Latest episode** works for M3U playlists as well as Xtream Codes ones (from the second refresh after updating).
- Your playlists, favourites and history are kept: the app updates its database in place.
