## What's new in 1.11.0

**Cast Dispatcharr live channels**

- Live channels from a Dispatcharr server (0.25 or newer) can now be cast to a Chromecast. Mosaic asks Dispatcharr for an MP4 version of the channel, which a Chromecast can play. Your phone keeps playing the normal stream.
- **Sound that works on the TV:** if you've connected your Dispatcharr login for recordings, Mosaic also asks Dispatcharr to convert the channel's audio to AAC while casting, using the built-in **Web Player (AAC Audio)** profile. Channels with Dolby or MP2 sound then don't play in silence. Your Dispatcharr user needs the *Standard* level or above.
- Each channel cast this way runs an ffmpeg process on your Dispatcharr server while you watch.

If you're on 1.10.1, this update installs straight over the top.
