## Tellycine 1.12.0: keep browsing while you watch

- **The player sits at the top on phones and tablets.** Held upright, what you're watching plays at the top of the app, and you can keep browsing below it: switch tabs, look through the guide or pick another channel, which plays in its place. Tap the video for its controls, including channel up and down and **✕** to stop.
- **Full screen when you turn it sideways**, or with the **full screen** button. Press it again, press Back or turn it upright to go back.
- **Picture-in-picture when you leave.** Go to your home screen while something's playing and it carries on in a small window. Before, this didn't always happen on Android 12 and later.
- **Fixed:** a crash in very small windows.
- On a TV, the player is unchanged.
