## Tellycine 1.13.0: more than one TV guide per playlist

- **Several XMLTV guides for a playlist.** In a playlist's **Advanced options**, press **+ Add another guide URL** for each guide you want, and **✕** to remove one. On a TV, use the **Add another guide URL** row. The **Add from phone** page has the same + button.
- **Replace or combine.** With a guide entered, tick **Also use the provider's guide** (or **…the guide linked in the playlist**) to keep the playlist's own guide and add yours to it, for example to fill in channels it's missing. Leave it unticked for your guides to replace it, as before.
- Guides you've already set keep working as they did. Your playlists, favourites and history are kept: the app updates its database in place.
