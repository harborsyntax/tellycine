## Tellycine 1.14.0: details from TMDB

- **Details from TMDB (optional).** Turn on **Details from TMDB** in Settings → Movies & series to fill in your movies and series from [TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org), The Movie Database. It's off unless you turn it on, as it sends the names of your titles to TMDB. Tellycine has its own TMDB key, so there's nothing else to set up.
- **On a title's page:** its tagline, TMDB's rating and the age rating, genres, the director and writers (or who created a series, and its network), the cast with photos, and the plot when your provider has none. Series show whether they're still running and when the next episode is out, each season's overview, and episode air dates, with names, plots and pictures where your provider has none.
- **More to watch:** a film's other parts and titles like the one you're looking at, as far as your playlists have them.
- **In the lists:** TMDB's ratings, posters for titles without one, and two more sorts: **Rating**, and for series **Last aired** (when the latest episode was broadcast).
- **Your own TMDB key (optional):** add one under **TMDB API key** in Settings to use it instead of Tellycine's.
- The Playlists page shows how looking titles up is going. The first look-up takes a while with a big playlist; after that, Tellycine only asks about what's new or due. Nothing from TMDB is kept for more than six months.
- **Air dates from TVmaze** has been replaced by details from TMDB, which includes air dates. If you had it on, turn on Details from TMDB for the **Last aired** sort.
- On a TV, the version and credits at the bottom of Settings can now be reached with the remote.

Your playlists, favourites and history are kept: the app updates its database in place.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
