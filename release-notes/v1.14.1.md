## Tellycine 1.14.1: quicker to the categories in the TV guide

- **Left gets to the channels sooner.** In the guide on Android TV, Left no longer steps back through the last two hours of programmes before reaching the channel names. It goes back in time only through programmes you can replay, on channels with catch-up. On other channels, Left from what's on now goes straight to the channel, and once more opens the categories. The same goes for the guide over the picture in the player.
