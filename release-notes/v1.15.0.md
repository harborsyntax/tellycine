## Tellycine 1.15.0: sound on more channels

- **Sound on more channels.** Some phones and TV boxes can't play the sound formats many channels and movies use, so they played without sound. Tellycine now plays Dolby Digital (AC-3), Dolby Digital Plus (E-AC-3), DTS, Dolby TrueHD and MPEG audio (MP1 and MP2, common on broadcast channels) itself when your device can't. Where it can, your device's own decoder is still used, and Dolby still goes to your TV or receiver.
- **Says why there's no sound.** If a channel's sound is in a format neither your device nor Tellycine can play, such as Dolby AC-4, the player says so and which format it is. On a phone, tap **Open with…** on the message to try another app, such as VLC. On a TV, **Open with…** is in the controls.

Tellycine plays these formats with [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org), licensed under the GNU LGPL 2.1 or later. Its credit and a link to its source are at the bottom of Settings. The APK here is about 5 MB bigger, as it has FFmpeg for every kind of processor. Google Play sends only the one your device needs, about 1.3 MB.
