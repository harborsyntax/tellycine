## Tellycine 1.16.0: quick while it refreshes

- **The app stays quick while playlists refresh.** Big TV guides (hundreds of thousands of programmes) used to make the app wait while they loaded, especially on TV boxes: the Movies and Series pages could stay empty, and favourites and other changes took a while to show. Guides now load in the background, at low priority, and the guide you see updates in one quick step once the new one is ready.
- **Less work.** A TV guide that hasn't changed since it was last loaded isn't loaded again. When the app starts, guides are refreshed when they're running out (less than a day left) or are two days old, rather than every 12 hours. Refreshing a playlist updates its guide only when it has new channels. **Refresh** and **Update TV guide only** still update it when you ask.

Your playlists, favourites and history are kept: the app updates its database in place.
