## Tellycine 1.16.1: back to your channel in the TV guide

- **Back from a channel lands on it in the guide.** On Android TV, going back to the guide from the player now highlights the channel you were watching, rather than the first channel in the guide. That includes a channel you changed to in the player, and the last channel when Tellycine opens on it as it starts.
- **Back in the guide opens the categories first.** Back from the guide now opens its categories, and Back again opens the side menu. Right from the menu returns to the channel you were on.
