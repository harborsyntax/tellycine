## Tellycine 1.17.0: the Sports Hub

- **A new Sports tab** (Sports in the TV menu) shows the sport on now and coming up in your playlists: football, Formula 1, MotoGP, golf, tennis, snooker, darts and boxing, and any other sport your guides call sport. It's found in your TV guides, and in event channels' names such as "EPL 03: Arsenal v Chelsea 15:00".
- **One event, every channel.** The same match on several channels, or in several playlists, is one event: tap it to choose where to watch, or watch it from the start where the channel has catch-up. On TV, OK on a live event plays it.
- **Your sports and teams first.** Choose the sports you follow, and teams and players to put first under For you, with their next event in Your teams. Tellycine knows UK teams, the F1 grid and the snooker, darts, tennis, golf and boxing names, and their other names ("Man Utd" is Manchester United).
- **No spoilers.** No scores or results are shown, and replays and highlights are hidden unless you turn them on.
- **Private.** It all happens on your device, from your own playlists: nothing is sent anywhere. Turn it off in Settings → Sports.
- **Quick.** Sport is looked for in the background after a refresh, and only in what's new in the guide.
- **TV:** a channel played from Search or the Library no longer keeps playing unseen after Back.

Guides are loaded again at their next refresh, to keep all their categories for the Sports Hub. Your playlists, favourites and history are kept.
