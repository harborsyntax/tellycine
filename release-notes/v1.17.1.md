## Tellycine 1.17.1: Back leaves from the menu again

- **Android TV: Back from the side menu leaves Tellycine again, and asks first.** Since 1.16.1, Back from the menu went back into the guide (to its categories), so it bounced between the two rather than leaving. Back now opens the menu from any screen as before (from the guide, its categories first; from Sports, its filters), and Back from the menu asks whether to leave: **Cancel** is chosen, so press Right then OK to leave, or Back to stay.
