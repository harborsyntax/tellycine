## Tellycine 1.17.2: better at finding live sport

The Sports Hub, tried on a full UK guide (Sky Sports, TNT Sports, Premier Sports, the BBC and ITV):

- **Live and replays told apart.** Sky Sports, TNT Sports and Premier Sports say "Live" on live coverage, so on their channels, matches that don't say it ("The Emirates FA Cup", "UEFA Champions League – Classics") are taken as replays, as are titles with a season or a past year ("EFL 26/27: …", "ENG v SA 2017"). "Hlts" means highlights. ITV and the BBC, which don't always say "Live", are as before.
- **Fewer shows taken for events.** Programmes only the category or the channel calls sport (club channels' features, news on Sky Sports News, "Sky Sports FC", interviews and legends) stay out, unless they say they're live or who's playing.
- **Who's playing, from the description:** "Plymouth Parkway v Berkhamsted (Kick-off 7.30pm). Coverage of…" names the match, for teams Tellycine doesn't know too.
- **Right sport:** Match of the Day's live matches are live, the Women's Super League and the NWSL are football, rugby league and American football aren't.

Sport is found again in your guides after the update.
