## Tellycine 1.18.0: Sports Hub filters, and its own mini player on TV

- **Live filter.** Live, beside All, shows the sport your TV guides say is live, now and to come, in every sport: no replays or shows that only might be.
- **Countries and competitions.** In a sport, a second row chooses between the competitions on: Premier League, Championship, FA Cup, Champions League, LaLiga and the rest, with a country (England, Scotland, Spain…) for all of its at once. Football goes by country, UK first; tennis, golf, darts and snooker by tour and event. Guides' many names for them are understood ("EPL", "The Emirates FA Cup", "Futbol Ligue 1"), and event channels' too ("UCL 04: …").
- **Fewer false finds.** Episodes of series ("The Simpsons – Tennis the Menace", "Starsky and Hutch – Shoot Out", anything with "S05 E12"), films, and songs on radio and music channels ("LOSE MY MIND (from F1 The Movie)") aren't taken for sport any more. On sports channels, series are left out as magazines.
- **TV: the guide's options in Sports.** An event's panel can **Record** it (where your playlist can record, as in the guide) and open it in **Multi-view** while it's on. Hold OK on any channel it's on for that channel's options and schedule, just as in the guide: favourites, Multi-view, Open with…, its stream URL, and watching or recording its programmes.
- **TV: the mini player in Sports.** Back from a channel played from the Sports Hub, it plays on at the top of the hub, as in the guide. OK on it, or holding Back, returns it to full screen. It stops when you leave Sports.
- **TV:** choosing another filter starts the hub from the top, where what's on now is.

Sport is found again in your guides after the update.
