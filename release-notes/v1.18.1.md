## Tellycine 1.18.1: saying what went wrong

- **Last problem.** If Tellycine closes unexpectedly, Settings → Problems → Last problem now says what went wrong and where: the error it couldn't handle, or that Android closed it while it was on screen, to free memory or because it stopped responding (Android 11 and later). On a TV, scan its QR code with your phone to copy it; on a phone, share it. Links, addresses and server names are left out, and it stays on your device unless you pass it on.
- **Sports Hub:** a team followed by two of its names ("Man Utd" and "Manchester United") has one card under Your teams, rather than closing the app.
