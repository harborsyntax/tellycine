## Tellycine 1.18.2: two crashes fixed

- **Catch-up no longer closes the app.** Watching a programme again from an M3U playlist's catch-up (in the guide, the channel's schedule or the Sports Hub) stopped Tellycine on Android: a pattern for the catch-up link that Android's stricter regular expressions refuse. Every pattern in Tellycine is now checked against Android's rules as it's built.
- **Choosing filters in the Sports Hub on TV no longer closes the app.** Each filter now has its events laid out afresh, from the top, rather than changing the rows already on screen as the focus moved into them, which could have Compose place one twice ("Place was called on a node which was placed already").
- **Last problem, readable.** Its QR code now gives your phone the text (it was empty on some phones), and on a TV the report is shown in full beside it, to photograph if you'd rather.
