## Tellycine 1.18.3: holding OK acts straight away

- **TV: holding OK opens options as soon as it's held**, as holding Back returns to the channel playing, without waiting for you to let go: a channel's options and schedule in the guide and channel list, an event's panel and its channels' options in the Sports Hub, and removing or deleting in the Library, Downloads and Recordings. Letting go afterwards presses nothing in what opened.
