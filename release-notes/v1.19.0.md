## Tellycine 1.19.0: search catch-up

- **Catch-up in search.** Programmes that have been on and are still in their channel's catch-up archive have a section of their own in search results, **Catch-up**, most recent first, and a filter of their own. Choose one to watch it again. They used to come after everything still to come, and a name with plenty still to come (such as the news) could crowd them out altogether.
- On phones and TV, in every playlist with catch-up.
