## Tellycine 1.2.0: mini player and compact guide on Android TV

- **Keep watching while you browse the guide.** Press Back from a live channel and it keeps playing in a mini player at the top of the guide. Hold Back, or press OK on the mini player, to return to it full screen without the stream restarting.
- **Compact guide.** The new Compact button at the top of the guide shrinks the rows so more channels fit on screen. Expand switches back, and your choice is remembered.
