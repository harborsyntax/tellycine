## What's new in 1.2.0

**Dispatcharr recordings (DVR)**

- **Connect:** open a Dispatcharr playlist's settings, tap **Connect** under *Dispatcharr recordings* and sign in with your Dispatcharr web login, or paste an API key. Mosaic keeps only the API key, never your password, and reuses your existing key so other apps that use it keep working.
- **Watch:** your recordings are in **Library › Recordings**, grouped into recording now, scheduled, recorded and failed. Finished recordings can be seeked, and ones still in progress play live.
- **Record:** long-press a channel and tap the record button next to any current or upcoming programme. Tap it again to cancel. Dispatcharr's pre/post padding settings apply.
- **Manage:** cancel scheduled recordings or delete finished ones from the Recordings screen.

Your Dispatcharr user needs DVR access: *view* to watch, *manage* to schedule and delete.
