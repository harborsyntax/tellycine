## Tellycine 1.20.0: one event, however it's named

The same match listed under different names, by channels in different countries and languages, is now one event in the Sports Hub.

- **More ways of naming a match.** American "Buffalo Bills at Minnesota Vikings", Portuguese "Flamengo x Palmeiras", French "Bills de Buffalo c. Vikings du Minnesota", German "gegen" and Spanish "contra" are read as matches, without what some guides add after the second side ("… NFL Football In-Game Live Monday Night Gameday", "New"). Football in other languages ("Fútbol", "Futebol") counts when a match is named. Names keep their accents: "Bayern München".
- **Names nearly the same are one.** Matches on at the same time whose sides are both the same team by another name are put together: "Bills" and "Buffalo Bills", "Bills de Buffalo" and "Buffalo Bills", "L Orient" and "Leyton Orient", "Bayern München" and "Bayern Munich". Not Manchester United and Manchester City, a women's or youth side and the senior one, or a fighter's two fights. Formula 1 and MotoGP sessions on at the same time are one whatever they're called, as are other events of the same name.
- **Same as another event…** On an event, choose the event it is too, and they're one from then on, as are the same teams' next matches under those names. Settings → Sports → Put together by hand forgets them.
- **Leave categories out.** Settings → Sports → Categories lists the categories sport was found in, the most first: untick one to leave its channels out of the Sports Hub, such as another country's local channels.

Sport is found again in your guides after the update.
