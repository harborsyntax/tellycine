## What's new in 1.3.0

- **Open with…:** long-press a channel and choose **Open with…** to play it in another app such as VLC or MX Player. Android's app chooser always appears, and the user agent and referrer are passed to apps that support them.
- **Share stream URL:** share a channel's stream URL from the same sheet. On Dispatcharr playlists you can share either the **Dispatcharr URL** or the **source URL** from the provider behind it. If a channel has several sources, you choose which one. Sharing a source URL needs your Dispatcharr login connected in the playlist settings.
- **Default player:** new setting under **Settings › Playback**. Choose the built-in player or an external app for everything you play. With an external app, Android lets you pick one to always use. Dispatcharr recordings still play in the built-in player, because they need a login header external apps can't send.

Shared stream URLs include your provider login details, so only share them with people you trust.
