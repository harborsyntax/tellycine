## Tellycine 1.3.0: guide over the picture, and stats for nerds

- **TV guide over the picture (Android TV).** The new TV guide button in the live player's controls shows the guide over the lower part of the screen, so the channel keeps playing while you browse. It opens on the channel you're watching; OK changes channel or replays a past programme, and Back closes it.
- **Stats for nerds (phones, tablets and TVs).** A new button in the player shows what's playing in detail: stream type, video and audio formats, HDR and colour information, decoders, download rate, buffer, dropped frames and the screen's display mode.
