## Tellycine 1.3.2: categories in the TV guide

- **Categories are now part of the guide (Android TV).** In both the main guide and the guide over the picture, the categories are a column to the left of the channels, shown faded while you browse. Press Left from the channel names to open it, then OK on a category to show its channels; Right or Back go back without changing.
- **Fixed:** reopening the guide over the picture after browsing another category could highlight the wrong channel.
