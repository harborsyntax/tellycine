## What's new in 1.4.0

**Chromecast support**

- **Cast button:** a cast button appears in the top bar and in the player whenever a Chromecast or Google TV is on your network.
- **Casting:** start casting and the current channel, catch-up programme, movie or episode moves to the TV from the same point. Use the player controls on your phone as a remote. Playback keeps going when you leave the app, with controls in the notification.
- **Stop casting:** playback returns to your phone where it left off.

**What can't be cast**

- Chromecasts can't play raw MPEG-TS streams. For standard Xtream Codes accounts, Mosaic automatically switches live channels to the provider's HLS stream. Dispatcharr only provides MPEG-TS, so its live channels may not play on a Chromecast.
- The TV fetches the stream itself, so channels that need a specific user agent or referrer may fail to cast.
- Dispatcharr recordings play on your phone only.
- Casting needs Google Play services on your phone.
