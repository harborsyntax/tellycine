## Tellycine 1.4.0: colour themes for Android TV

- **Choose the TV interface's colours.** Settings → Appearance → Theme has five dark themes made for the big screen: **Warm** (the original look), **Midnight** (cool white and sky blue on blue-black), **Graphite** (pure white and teal on neutral grey), **Forest** (soft green on deep green) and **High contrast** (white and yellow on black, easiest to read from across the room). A theme applies as soon as you pick it, across the whole TV interface.
