## What's new in 1.4.2

**Updates now install over the previous version.** Releases are now signed with a permanent Mosaic key. Earlier releases each had a different signature, which is why Android said "App not installed" when updating.

> **One last time:** uninstall your current Mosaic before installing 1.4.2, because the older builds were signed differently. From 1.4.2 onwards, new versions install straight over the top and keep your playlists and settings.
