## What's new in 1.5.0

**Library sorting and grouping**

- **Sort:** tap the sort icon at the top of the Library. Choose **most recent**, **name A–Z**, **name Z–A** or **playlist order**. Playlist order uses channel numbers, then the playlist's own order. Mosaic remembers your choice.
- **Group by category:** turn this on in the same menu to split your favourite channels, movies and series, recently watched channels and continue watching into one row per category. Rows follow your playlist's category order.

If you're on 1.4.2, this update installs straight over the top.
