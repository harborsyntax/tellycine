## Tellycine 1.5.0: multi-view for Android TV

- **Watch up to four live channels at once.** Choose **Multi-view** from the player's controls, or hold OK on a channel in the guide or channel list. Multi-view opens with that channel and the guide, ready to pick the next. It never opens more channels from a playlist than its connection limit allows.
- **Sound per channel.** Press OK on a channel to turn its sound on or off, hear only that channel, or make it quieter or louder. You can hear more than one channel at once.
- **Layouts.** **Grid** shows every channel the same size. **Side column** shows one channel big with the others in a column beside it, and **Bottom row** puts the others in a row below. **Enlarge** makes a small channel the big one. Multi-view remembers the layout you used last.
- **Channel numbers are no longer shown** in the guide and channel lists, on TV and phone, leaving more room for channel names and programmes. Typing a number on the remote still goes to that channel.
