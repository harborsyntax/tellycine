## Tellycine 1.6.0: ready for Google Play

- **Updated for Android 16.** Tellycine now targets Android 16 (API 36), as Google Play requires. On Android 16 devices, Back works as before, including holding Back on a TV to return the mini player's channel to full screen.
- **An app bundle for Google Play** (`tellycine-v1.6.0.aab`) comes with each release from now on, alongside the APK.
- **A fuller privacy policy** in the documentation, covering what stays on your device, who Tellycine connects to, and Google Cast.
