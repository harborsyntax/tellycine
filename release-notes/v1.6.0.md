## What's new in 1.6.0

**Search the TV guide**

- Search now finds programmes in the TV guide as well as channels, movies and series. Use the new **TV guide** filter to see only programmes.
- Results show the time and channel. Programmes on now come first, then upcoming ones, then past ones you can still catch up on.
- Tap a programme that's on now or coming up to watch the channel live. Tap a past one to replay it.

**The Mosaic logo on every tab**

- Live TV, Movies, Series and Library now show the Mosaic logo at the top, like the Playlists tab. The active playlist's name, and the playlist switcher, sit underneath.

**Documentation**

- A full user guide with screenshots is now in the repository's [`docs`](https://github.com/mrjack711/mosaic/blob/main/docs/Home.md) folder.

If you're on 1.5.0, this update installs straight over the top.
