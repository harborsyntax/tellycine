## Tellycine 1.7.0: richer movie and series pages, search everywhere

- **Movie and series pages show more artwork.** On a TV the backdrop fills the screen. When the provider has several backdrops, they take turns, fading from one to the next with a slow zoom (no movement with Android's Remove animations setting on). Without a backdrop, a blurred copy of the poster is used instead of a stretched one.
- **Pages take their colours from the artwork**, in the app's light or dark look. The High contrast TV theme stays as it is.
- **Season posters:** when the provider has them, seasons are shown as posters to choose from.
- **Trailers:** a Trailer button opens the provider's trailer in the YouTube app.
- **Search looks in all your playlists**, and each result shows which playlist it's from.
- **The TV guide uses the whole screen.** The side menu hides while the guide is showing (Back still opens it), the categories column tucks into a thin edge on the left, and Compact and List are icon buttons, so the programme details run across the top and about half an hour more of the guide fits on screen.
- **Fixed:** long programmes in the guide stopped short and left an empty space until you scrolled back to their start. They now fill their row on TV, over the player and on phones.
