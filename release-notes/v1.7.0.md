## What's new in 1.7.0

**Downloads: watch movies and episodes offline**

- **Download:** tap the download button next to the heart on a movie, or on any episode of a series. Downloads run in the background, even with Mosaic closed, with Android's progress notification.
- **Watch:** press Play as usual. Downloaded titles play from your device, with no connection needed, and keep your resume point in step with streaming.
- **Manage:** open **Library → Downloads** or **Settings → Manage downloads** to see storage used and free, follow progress, retry, cancel, play or delete downloads, or delete them all.
- **Wi-Fi only:** downloads wait for Wi-Fi by default. Change this in **Settings → Download over Wi-Fi only**.
- Titles your provider only offers as HLS or DASH streams can't be downloaded. Downloads are removed if you uninstall Mosaic.

**Fixes**

- Movie and series pages without background art no longer show a stray grey dot.

If you're on 1.6.1, this update installs straight over the top.
