## Tellycine 1.8.0: browse your playlists one at a time or all together

- **A playlist filter in Live TV, Movies and Series.** With more than one playlist, choose **All playlists** or one playlist. On a TV the choice is at the top of the guide's categories column and of the categories panels; on a phone or tablet it's beside the categories. Each section keeps its own choice, and **Use this playlist** on a playlist card goes back to that one playlist everywhere.
- **All playlists together:** categories with the same name become one (News from two playlists is a single News category), favourites come from every playlist, and each channel, movie and series shows which playlist it's from. Catch-up, recording and changing channel use the item's own playlist.
- **Details say which playlist an item is from**, on movie and series pages, above the TV guide, in a channel's options and in the phone's channel sheet, whenever you have more than one playlist.
