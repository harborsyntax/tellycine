## What's new in 1.8.0

**Choose a user agent**

- Some providers only serve streams to apps they recognise. In **Edit playlist → Advanced options → User agent**, pick from common apps: TiviMate, IPTV Smarters Pro, OTT Navigator, Perfect Player, Kodi, MX Player, ExoPlayer, FFmpeg, Chrome on Android or a Samsung smart TV.
- Choose **Custom…** to enter your own. It starts from the preset you had selected, so you can tweak it.
- The user agent is used for the playlist, guide, playback and downloads.

**Maximum connections**

- The playlist card shows how many streams the account can play at once.
- Xtream Codes accounts fill this in automatically. For M3U playlists, or to override it, enter it in **Edit playlist → Maximum connections**.

If you're on 1.7.0, this update installs straight over the top.
