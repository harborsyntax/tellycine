## Tellycine 1.9.0: sort the TV guide's categories, and a fuller guide over the player

- **A Sort button at the top of the TV guide's categories.** Choose **Provider order** (as your provider lists them) or **A–Z**: press OK on Sort to switch. The order is remembered, and the categories panel in the channel list has the same button.
- **The guide over the player works like the main guide.** It shows the same playlists, with **All playlists** or one playlist above its categories, the same Sort button, and which playlist the highlighted channel is from. It still opens on the channel you're watching, in its category, even when Live TV is showing another playlist. Choosing playlists or a sort there changes them in Live TV too. This also applies to the guide in multi-view.
