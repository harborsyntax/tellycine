## What's new in 1.9.0

**Tidy up your Library**

- **Long-press to remove:** long-press anything in the Library to remove it from **favourites**, **recently watched** or **continue watching**, depending on the row.
- **Undo:** changed your mind? Tap **Undo** in the message at the bottom of the screen to put it back.
- **Clear all:** **Continue watching** and **Recently watched channels** now have a **Clear all** button, with a confirmation first. Favourites, downloads and finished episodes aren't affected.

If you're on 1.8.0, this update installs straight over the top.
