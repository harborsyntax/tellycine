## What's new in 1.9.1

**Fixes**

- **No more repeated programmes in the TV guide.** Channels matched to the guide by name could pick up several guide channels with the same name, such as regional or HD versions, and show the same programme more than once. Each channel now uses one guide channel, preferring an exact `tvg-id` match, and repeated programmes are skipped.
- Your TV guides re-import automatically the next time you open Mosaic. If **Refresh on launch** is off, use **⋮ → Update TV guide only** on the playlist.

If you're on 1.9.0, this update installs straight over the top.
