## Tellycine 1.9.1: faster scrolling on TV, and a fix for series pages

- **Hold Up or Down to scroll faster on TV.** In the guide, the channel list, categories, movies, series, a channel's schedule and the player's channel list, holding Up or Down starts one row at a time and speeds up the longer you hold. Held, it stops at the top or bottom of the list; press again to move on from there.
- **Fixed:** on a TV, the top of a series page (its title and details) could be cut off when the page opened. The page now opens at its top, and returns there whenever you move back up to its buttons.
