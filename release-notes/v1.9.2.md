## Tellycine 1.9.2: a quicker guide with big or combined playlists

- **The guide opens faster**, most noticeably with **All playlists** or one very large playlist. Opening the channel list used to work out what's on now for every channel, just to count them; now it only does that for the channels you can see. In our tests with 16,000 channels, the guide's list loads about four to five times faster.
- **No more reloading every minute.** The guide used to reload its whole list each minute to keep "what's on now" up to date. Now each channel on screen moves on to its next programme when the current one ends, and the list reloads only when a playlist's TV guide is updated.
- **A smoother start.** Playlists that need refreshing when the app opens now start a few seconds later, so the first screen opens without a download competing with it, and the playlist you're using goes first.
